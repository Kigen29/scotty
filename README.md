# Scotty

I want to build an agent /agents for my business . I am software developer in Kenya and I want to seek out other businesses that are in need of my services . We offer system development , mobile app development and other Software Engineering activities . We make websites for small businesses especially to establish their online presence , make their work in inventory management, client acquisition , analytics even SEO among other business operations easier. For example we have built lakevictoriaaquaculture.com , heartbeestsafaris.com , rangautiles.com , itchyfeet.co.ke among many others. So I want an agent or a group of agents that work together to research businesses in Kenya with no WEBSITES , OR SYSTEMS FOR THEIR BUSINESSES and cold email them , establish a conversation with the business owners to see if they are interested, so basically its like a scouting regiment to discover these types of businesses and then write emails offering our services to them that uniquely solves that business issues /problems and establish a convo with them , then I just get reports of the businesses that have been contacted , the ones that have responded , those yet to respond and so forth and make this system autonomous.
